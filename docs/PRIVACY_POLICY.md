# Pencilmark Sudoku — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-11_

Pencilmark Sudoku ("the app") is an offline puzzle game.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data.

**What is stored on your device:** your settings, game progress, statistics, achievements and learning progress. They are kept in the app's private storage on your device and are never sent anywhere.

**Network access:** the app does not make network requests for gameplay, hints, puzzles or progress.

**Third parties:** the app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.

**Deleting your data:** use Settings → Reset statistics, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

**Children:** the app collects no data from anyone, including children.
