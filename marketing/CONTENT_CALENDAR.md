## 1. Rules that apply to every row

- **CTA before launch:** "Coming soon to Google Play". No store badge, no link to a listing that is not live.
- **CTA after launch:** marked **TODO (post-launch)** — e.g. "Now on Google Play" + official badge per Google's badge
  guidelines. Use only once the listing is live and the owner approves.
- **Do not claim "Free"** until the owner confirms pricing. Then it can be added to captions and end cards.
- **No** ratings, download counts, reviews, "#1" or award claims. No fake comments, no engagement pods, no paid fake
  followers.
- Stats/achievements screenshots are **scripted demo data** — keep the "Demo data" label wherever they appear.
- Daily challenge posts never show a future daily, and never show today's solution on the same day.
- Every deduction in a caption must match its engine example (see the concept in `VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md`). Marketing boards
  are fixture example #0 or positions mined from other techniques' practice puzzles, never a lesson's exercise or
  mastery positions (fixture #1–#3), so posts don't spoil lessons.

## 6. Community etiquette (Reddit and similar)

- **Value first.** Most contributions should be genuine help: answering technique questions, explaining a deduction,
  sharing a worked example. Target: no more than 1 in 10 contributions mentions the app.
- **Read each subreddit's rules and sidebar** before posting; rules change. Some ban self-promotion, some allow it only
  in specific threads or on specific days. If unclear, message the moderators first, or skip.
- **Disclose** whenever the app is mentioned: "I'm the developer of Pencilmark."
- **No link-dropping.** Don't paste links into unrelated threads or answer questions with "use my app". Link only
  where the rules allow and someone would reasonably want it.
- **No vote manipulation**, alt accounts, or asking friends to upvote.
- Technique answers can use the real engine boards from `marketing/data/` (cite the position, explain in words); don't
  post invented positions.
- Accept criticism publicly and politely; note bug reports for the owner.

## 8. Metrics to watch

The app itself has **no analytics or tracking**, so all measurement is platform-side.
